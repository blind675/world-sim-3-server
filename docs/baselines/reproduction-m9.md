# Milestone 9 reproduction capacity run

The [JSON report](reproduction-m9.json) comes from `npm run baseline:births -- --output docs/baselines/reproduction-m9.json`. It uses the standard seeds 20260420, 20260421, and 20260422 with 50 founding agents each. The runner advances the saved clock directly to tick 10,799, one tick before the template's 30-day adult threshold, then runs 360 ordinary simulation ticks. Positions and needs are still those of fresh agents. This controlled setup exercises pairing and capacity without spending 30 simulated days reaching adulthood; it does **not** measure natural long-run survival or a stable birth/death balance.

| 20260420 | 10 | 60 / 60 | 4 | 222 | 1.6 / 0.8 | 245 ms | 212 MiB | 0.98 MiB |
| 20260421 | 10 | 60 / 60 | 2 | 216 | 1.6 / 0.8 | 287 ms | 300 MiB | 1.11 MiB |
| 20260422 | 10 | 60 / 60 | 3 | 215 | 1.6 / 0.8 | 357 ms | 341 MiB | 1.16 MiB |

All three runs reached the hard cap at the first adult check, and none exceeded it during the following day. The parental cost sums are normalized needs units across all births (0.08 hunger and 0.04 thirst to **each** of two parents). Food servings count completed calls to consume food; drinking-state ticks count agent ticks, not separate water sources. The test world began with about 34 KiB in its primary snapshot and ended with 0.95–1.13 MiB. Most of that increase is ordinary memory/path growth across 360 ticks, alongside ten newborn agents; it is not an isolated per-birth storage cost. Primary plus previous backup remained 0.98–1.16 MiB. The three seeds run sequentially in one process, so later RSS peaks may include retained allocations from earlier seeds. Concurrent benchmark/check activity also affects tick timing. These are capacity signals, not live Railway measurements.

An additional [2,500-tick capped run](reproduction-m9-long.json) used seed 20260420 with the same synthetic age advance. It kept 60/60 agents alive and at or below the cap, consumed 230 food servings, and recorded 1,418 drinking-state agent ticks. Tick p95 was 204 ms, peak sampled RSS was 356 MiB, and the final primary snapshot was 1.75 MiB. Its backup is the tiny initial checkpoint, so the 1.78 MiB pair is smaller than a production pair with two mature snapshots. This longer run provides a more relevant memory check near the cap; it still starts with fresh positions and needs at day 30.

The [standard 2,500-tick survival comparison](survival-m9.md) covers the pre-adult period against milestone 8. Milestone 10 still needs many naturally simulated days and observed births and deaths before choosing a production population balance. Age-related death remains disabled in milestone 9. The existing single instance, persistent volume, and 512 MiB starting memory target remain plausible from these offline samples; monitor Railway RSS and snapshots after release, and increase memory or lower the cap if sustained usage approaches the limit.
